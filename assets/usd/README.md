`../mjcf/piper_mujoco/PIPER.xml` with MuJoCo USD Converter 0.2.0 for
Isaac Sim-compatible visualization and integration.

See `../mjcf/piper_mujoco/LICENSE` and the repository-level
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

The generated USD is provided as-is. Regeneration may produce different
serialization or metadata depending on the converter and USD versions.
